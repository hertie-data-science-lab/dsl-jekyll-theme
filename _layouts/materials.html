Three things can be true of a session here, and the page shows whichever apply: reading_list the citation text the teaching team wrote, inlined verbatim from the released `readings/NN_.../` folder. This is the reading LIST - authors, titles, chapters - and it is public. "reading -" links to the reading files themselves. They point into the cohort's PRIVATE materials repo, so they resolve only for enrolled students: this site repo is public, and hosting the readings here would publish copyrighted material to the open internet. readings_pending the plan ships readings for this session but they have not landed yet. A session with none of the three simply doesn't appear - a lecture-only week would otherwise sit here reading "not released yet" for the whole term.
