--- layout: page --- {{ content }} {% comment %} Readings, session by session. Three things can be true of a session here, and the page shows whichever apply: reading_list the citation text the teaching team wrote, inlined verbatim from the released `readings/NN_.../` folder. This is the reading LIST - authors, titles, chapters - and it is public. "reading -" links to the reading files themselves. On a COHORT site they point into the private materials repo, so only enrolled members can open one: that site is public, and hosting the readings there would publish copyrighted material to the open internet. On the public open-courseware site they are hosted deliberately. Which of the two it is decides the page's own intro line, so this layout does not claim either. readings_pending the plan ships readings for this session but they have not landed yet. A session with none of the three simply doesn't appear - a lecture-only week would otherwise sit here reading "not released yet" for the whole term. This lives in the theme rather than in each site's own page so that a change reaches every cohort site at once (see the toolkit's site.py, which owns the page stub).
